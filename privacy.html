Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

SariSpin respects the privacy of customers who use this website to request laundry services. This policy explains what information the current website collects, how it is used and stored, and how you can request its deletion.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a laundry service request, the website may collect:

  • Your full name.
  • Your mobile number.
  • Your selected laundry service.
  • Your preferred service date.
  • Your address, if you request pickup and delivery.
  • Any additional notes you choose to provide.

Please provide only information needed to arrange your service. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through the request form.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information is intended to help SariSpin:

  • Review and organize laundry service requests.
  • Contact customers to confirm their preferred date and service details.
  • Arrange pickup and delivery when requested.
  • Contact customers when their laundry is ready for collection.
  • Keep track of requests marked as completed.

Submitting a preferred date does not guarantee an appointment. SariSpin must contact you to confirm the request.

3. How Information Is Stored

The current version of this website stores submitted requests in your browser's local storage. The website code does not send these request records to a shared database or server.

Because of this, a request submitted in one browser or device will not automatically appear in the owner dashboard opened in another browser or device. Clearing the browser's site data may also remove saved requests.

The owner dashboard in this version is not password-protected. Anyone who can access the dashboard in the same browser may be able to view or delete its saved requests. Do not use this demonstration version to store real customer information unless appropriate access controls and data-handling arrangements have been established.

4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The current website code does not include a third-party database, analytics integration, or automatic SMS-sending service for submitted requests. SariSpin may use the contact information you provide to communicate with you about your requested service.

The website's hosting provider may process technical information as part of delivering the website. The handling of such information depends on the hosting provider's own policies and configuration.

5. Retention and Deletion

Requests saved by this version remain in the browser's local storage until they are deleted or the relevant site data is cleared. The owner dashboard provides a Delete Request control for removing an individual saved request from that browser.

You may contact SariSpin to ask about a request or request deletion. Because this version does not use a shared database, a request may exist only in the browser where it was submitted.

6. Safeguards and Limitations

The website validates required form fields and escapes submitted text before displaying it in the dashboard. However, these measures do not make the website fully secure. Local browser storage is not a secure customer database, and the current dashboard has no authentication system.

Please avoid submitting real personal information when using the website as a school demonstration. Use fictitious test information instead.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of information related to a service request, contact SariSpin.
